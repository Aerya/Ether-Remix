# Ether v0.3.22

Highlights:

- **TheTVDB, and Ether without TMDB.** TheTVDB is now a full catalogue alongside TMDB, for movies, shows, people, artwork and every episode order. Each library chooses where its details come from, and each show can use a different episode order. Either catalogue can be turned off: Ether starts, sets up and plays with TMDB, TheTVDB or neither (#817, thanks @Pokipoki).
- **Live TV catch-up.** When your IPTV provider keeps an archive (Xtream `tv_archive`, M3U `catchup`), programmes that already aired play from the guide in Vela. They also appear in a new **Catch-up** library in Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and Silo, so last night's news is one click away in any app (#812, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Rewind live TV.** Every live IPTV channel keeps a rolling rewind window while someone is watching (60 minutes by default). In Vela you can pause, rewind, start the current programme over and jump back to live.
- **What's new after every update.** Ether shows the release notes in the console the first time you open it after an update, so you don't have to look them up on GitHub.
- **Refresh metadata and Fix match** for any title, in the console and from Plex and Jellyfin/Emby apps. Fix match moves watch history, favourites, collections and saved releases to the right title.
- **Collections rows.** One Home row of hand-picked collections shown as cards, portrait or landscape, with their own artwork (#816, thanks @xSkibbles).
- **Test speed** for every Usenet and debrid provider: ten seconds of real downloads and a plain answer to "is it fast enough for my streams?".
- **Playback that keeps going.** An Auto watch continues on another cached copy when a debrid provider loses the file (#823, thanks @theatarr), and a busy transcoder picks a version that plays without conversion instead of refusing the play.
- **Faster Vela library collections** on large libraries: one page went from thousands of database statements to about twenty (#814, thanks @krakenn9).

## Metadata

- **Catalogues:**
  - **Integrations › Catalogues** lists TMDB and TheTVDB as equal cards. TheTVDB uses your own free API key. TMDB keeps its built-in key and can be turned off; adding your own key or **Turn on** brings it back.
  - **Setup** asks where movie and TV details should come from, and can be skipped.
  - With TMDB off, features only TMDB provides (its charts and lists, episode groups) are hidden, and the console says why. Viewers never see an error.
- **Details from:** each library chooses its catalogue. A show's titles, artwork, people and episode list come from that choice.
- **Episode order:**
  - **The setting:** each show's **…** menu has **Episode order**: the library default (shown with its source), TMDB aired, TMDB episode groups, or TheTVDB official, DVD or absolute.
  - **Where it applies:** the order is the same in every app, and release search uses the same numbers (`tvdbid` with TheTVDB numbering).
  - **History is kept:** watch history and progress follow the episode itself, so switching orders keeps them.
  - **Suggestions:** when the other catalogue lists more episodes for a show, its drawer suggests switching.
  - **Shows in several libraries:** a show's own setting wins, otherwise the first library on the Libraries page that holds it.
- **Shows TMDB doesn't have** can come from TheTVDB alone. When TheTVDB later turns out to match a TMDB show, the two become one title, and anything recorded in between moves across.
- **Refresh metadata** re-reads a title, its seasons and episodes from its catalogue and clears cached artwork everywhere. A season where only some episodes had been read no longer counts as complete, which caused shows to list only their first episode.
- **Fix match** searches TMDB and TheTVDB, marks the current match, and moves watch history, favourites, collections, pinned releases and stored NZBs to the chosen title in one step. Plex's **Fix match** and Jellyfin/Emby's **Identify** use it too.

## Live TV

- **Catch-up:**
  - **Detection:** archive windows are read during the normal source refresh, from Xtream `tv_archive` / `tv_archive_duration` and from M3U `catchup`, `catchup-days` and `catchup-source`. A channel loses catch-up when its provider stops reporting it.
  - **Playback:** a programme inside the archive plays as a normal, seekable title. It counts as one stream for the viewer and one connection to the provider, like live TV.
  - **Catch-up library:** Plex sees a TV section, with one show per programme and the airings as dated episodes. Jellyfin and Emby see Series and Episodes, and Silo sees the same library. Airings leave the library as they age out of the provider's archive.
  - **Channel badge:** channels with an archive show a **Catch-up: N days** badge in the console.
  - **Xtream:** Ether's own Xtream output now reports `tv_archive` and plays archived programmes, so Xtream apps get catch-up through Ether too.
- **Rewind:**
  - **Live TV › Settings** has one setting, "Rewind live TV up to N minutes" (0 to 240, default 60; 0 keeps live only).
  - The window only exists while a channel is being watched, and it stays within the transcode cache's disk budget. A full disk shortens the oldest windows instead of stopping playback.
  - A channel's own rewind length still takes precedence.
  - In Vela, a paused live channel keeps your place. **Live** jumps back to the live edge, and seeking outside the window says so instead of guessing.

## Home and collections

- **Collections rows:** in Recommendations, a **Collections** row shows chosen collections as cards. Pick and order them, or match them by name, and choose posters or landscape cards. Each card uses its collection's artwork and opens it. Collections imported from Stremio add-ons can be picked too. Jellyfin and Emby show the row as collections, Silo as collection posters, and Vela natively.

## Console

- **Integrations › Catalogues** cards match the rest of Integrations: each has its switch in the corner. Plugin and Stremio catalogue sources can be turned off without being uninstalled.
- **What's new:**
  - **When it shows:** after any update (automatic, installer, package or Docker), a drawer shows that version's release notes, built into Ether. Dismiss it once; it remembers.
  - **Updates page:** **Maintenance › Updates** reopens the installed version's notes, and an available update's notes show before you install it.
- **Update checks** no longer run every time you open Maintenance › Updates. The page shows when Ether last checked and when it checks next, and **Check now** checks on demand.
- **Test speed:** each Usenet and debrid provider has **Test speed** next to **Test**:
  - **Usenet:** about ten seconds of real article downloads from an NZB already in the store, over the provider's connections. Only spare connections are used while people are streaming.
  - **Debrid:** a short read of a recently played file.
  - **The result:** one verdict ("Too slow for your recent streams" or "Fast enough"), the speed in the drawer, and the details: speed per connection, connections refused and missing articles. Support bundles include the latest results and recent Usenet source and media rates.
- **Server load** (Sources › Usenet): **Balanced** shares requests across every non-backfill account instead of filling the first one. It suits several comparable unlimited accounts. An account that keeps missing a file's articles is asked after the others for that file. **In order** stays the default (#818, thanks @zab1996).
- **Playback** shows how many video conversions can run at once.

## Playback and apps

- **Downloads in Jellyfin and Emby apps** (Infuse and others): movies and episodes can be downloaded when the viewer is allowed to download, the same permission and limits as Plex. The original file is served with resume support and no conversion.
- **A release the indexer won't hand out again** ("already grabbed", grab limits) is set aside for its window and the next release plays, instead of the play failing. App error messages no longer contain indexer download links.
- **Music tracks** show as "Artist – Track" in Activity, sessions and history, not as an "S00E00" episode.
- **Jellyfin for Roku** no longer exits to Roku Home when you open a film or episode whose tracks Ether hasn't read yet. Episodes always carry their series, and the encoding settings endpoint answers like Jellyfin (#421, thanks @krakenn9).
- **Dolby Vision on Vela:** when Vela says the display has no Dolby Vision, or an owner rule turns it off, Ether no longer sends a Dolby Vision stream:
  - profile 5 converts;
  - profile 8.1 plays its HDR10 base layer.

  The same rules now apply when the original file plays directly (#810, thanks @theatarr).

- **Silo and on-demand Usenet:** a start whose release turns out unreadable partway through fails over to another release, instead of ending playback (#806, thanks @red4711).
- **When a provider loses the file:** an Auto or remembered watch whose debrid file is confirmed gone continues on another cached copy from the releases Ether already found: same edition, matching runtime, same position, and audio and subtitles matched by language. A temporary refusal or timeout doesn't count as gone. A version you picked yourself stays pinned and says it's unavailable (#823, thanks @theatarr).
- **When the transcoder is busy:** if every conversion slot is in use, Ether plays a version that needs no video conversion. If none exists, the play waits briefly in line, and background work gives way first. If it still can't start, the viewer is told the server is busy converting for other viewers, and Activity shows who holds the slots.
- **Switching versions** during playback makes the same decision as starting that version fresh, and keeps your position, audio and subtitles (#824, thanks @theatarr).
- **Plex for Fire TV and other Plex apps** offer the choice between your own Plex server's copy and Ether's again: search results now carry the identity Plex uses to merge sources (#819, thanks @eddieaoneill-sketch).
- **Plex for Android** plays FLAC music directly instead of converting it.
- **Downloads in Vela:** Vela can download originals for offline viewing, with the same download permission and daily limits as Plex and Jellyfin/Emby downloads (one shared quota). A paused download resumes where it stopped.
- **Offline progress:** progress watched offline (Vela, and Plex apps' offline sync) is applied only when it's newer than what the server has, never un-watches a finished title, and reaches Trakt, Simkl and Plex watch sync with the time it was watched.
- **Lyrics in Jellyfin, Plex and Emby apps:** music plugins that provide lyrics, synced or plain, now show them in Jellyfin (10.9+), Plex and Emby apps too, not only in Vela (#827, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).
- **Dolby Vision on Vela** uses the profiles a device declares when Ether has no profile list for it, so Android devices keep Dolby Vision. An explicit "no Dolby Vision" still wins (#821, thanks @theatarr).
- **Plex play queues** and pre-play details no longer wait for a whole source search before answering.
- **Vela Home:** a cached Home no longer lists a row that "See all" would refuse, so it can't loop on "Try again".

## Usenet and indexers

- **Debrid reads:** when Ether races two requests for the same piece of a file, only one that returns the right bytes can win. A fast error no longer cancels a healthy request (#825, thanks @theatarr).
- **Cached-only search:** a torrent an indexer reports late (after the cache-check window) is kept as "unknown" and can still play, instead of being dropped, even when it's the only cached one (#826, thanks @theatarr).
- **IPTV refreshes keep searches:** an hourly IPTV catalogue refresh that changed nothing no longer makes Ether forget its indexer searches, which had been using up indexers' daily API budgets (#828, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).
- **A wrong article from a backbone** (one that returns another file's article under a valid message ID) is treated as missing there, and the next backbone is tried, instead of corrupting the stream (#806).
- **A paused indexer is named** when it is why a play couldn't search, in every app.
- **Background and plugin warm-up searches** have their own back-off, so they can't pause an indexer for real plays, and they give way to playback.

## Live TV reliability

- **A provider's programme change** splices cleanly into the stream. Before, the change could be missed, so playback fell back to the slate and then re-encoded.

## Releases

- **A release named only "The.S01E03…"** (a show name cut down to an article) no longer counts as another show just because an indexer tagged it with that show's IDs. The same applies to other names that are only articles, or only the first word of the wanted title. Titles that differ only by a year or country, like "Doctor Who (2005)", still match.
- **Long-running shows** whose releases carry a later year (such as some Korean variety shows) are no longer rejected as "another show's premiere year" when the title and episode match.

## Plugins and the SDK

- **Plugins get NZBs only through Ether:**
  - **One store:** every NZB a plugin needs (search results, Usenet checks, playback) goes through Ether's indexer handling and its NZB store, the same path movies and TV use. An NZB is fetched from an indexer once and kept for good; repeat plays, retries, other viewers and other plugins read the stored copy.
  - **Refusals handled once:** grab limits and refusals are handled centrally.
  - **No direct indexer access:** plugins get release references, not indexer download links, and can't contact indexers directly.
  - **No plugin updates needed:** existing plugins keep working unchanged.
  - **Albums:** the tracks of an album share its one stored NZB instead of each asking the indexer again (#815, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Plugin plays reopen stored NZBs:** a track or book played before opens from the NZB store, even after its saved source expired, instead of searching indexers again.
- **Remote and sidecar plugins** receive release references and plain magnet hashes, never indexer links or keys, and errors reach them as fixed categories.
- **Plugin catalogue sync** never holds the database writer for long: on a 200,000-item music catalogue each write stays around 200 ms, so sign-ins and playback never wait behind a sync.
- **Watch sync for plugins** (#813, thanks @b4skyx):
  - `viewers.setWatchState` now works, marking a plugin's own items or catalogue titles watched or unwatched.
  - Watch events say what changed, why (playback, a manual mark, a plugin or an import), and which item: the plugin's own item reference, or catalogue IDs with season and episode.
  - A plugin's own writes are not echoed back to it.
- **`kinds.browse` filters** (#807, thanks @FizzWhirl):
  - `empty` selects items with no value in a field, or with any value;
  - an `anyGroups` group can match across fields ("label or parent label");
  - `excludeQuery` drops items whose text matches.
- **Faster multi-facet browses** (#808, thanks @FizzWhirl): a browse with several facets now starts from its narrowest one, for both the page and its count. On a 200,000-item kind it went from about 600 ms to 8 ms on SQLite, and from 216 ms to 13 ms on PostgreSQL.

## Trakt

- **A final scrobble that fails** (a 503 or a network error at the end of a film or episode) is retried, so the play isn't lost. A stop whose response was lost is recognised and isn't counted twice (#820, thanks @theatarr).

## Backups

- **Backups include the NZB store** by default. Local snapshots reuse unchanged packs, and restores stream and check every pack, with no size cap.
- **A free-space floor** (5% of the disk, between 2 GiB and 20 GiB) stops a backup or restore from filling the disk. It reports how much more space is needed and removes what it wrote.

## Performance

- **Plex version IDs:** the in-memory map of Plex version IDs is capped on every server, so it no longer grows without limit, and Plex episode lists read and write those IDs in one batch per page instead of six rows per episode (#829, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Catalogue snapshots** and the title-search index read only movies and shows when splitting their work into chunks (#830, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Vela library collections** batch their counts, membership and artwork reads, so a page costs about the same whatever the number of collections (#814).
- **Person pages** no longer wait on database writes while the server is busy (#814).

## Upgrade notes

- **Catch-up** appears on its own wherever a source reports an archive; there's nothing to turn on. It needs the provider to keep an archive for the channel.
- **Rewind** uses disk space while a channel is being watched, about 3 GB per viewer-hour at typical bitrates, within the existing transcode cache budget. Set it to 0 under Live TV › Settings to keep live only.
- **Update checks** on the Updates page are now on demand; Ether still checks on its own schedule.
- **TheTVDB** needs your own free key from thetvdb.com under Integrations › Catalogues. TMDB keeps working as before unless you turn it off.
- **Existing titles keep their IDs.** Choosing TheTVDB for a library or a show changes its episode list and numbering, not its watch history.
- **Vela 0.2.70** or later is recommended.
