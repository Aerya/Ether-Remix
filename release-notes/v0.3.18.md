# Ether v0.3.18

Highlights:

- **Live sports and event add-ons in Live TV.** Stremio add-ons that list live events under their own type (such as `sport`) now import as Live TV. Each of the add-on's lists (Live Now, Football, Hockey…) becomes a channel, its matches show in the guide, and tuning a channel plays the match that is live, moving on to the next live match if a stream fails. Ether refreshes the match lists only while someone is using the source.
- **Books, music and audiobooks play and read everywhere.** Plugin ebooks open in Jellyfin's reader (`/Items/{id}/Download`) and show a Read action in Vela, and opening a book runs the plugin's search. Vela web plays MP3, FLAC, Opus and other audio directly, and audio that needs converting starts reliably. Jellyfin album pages list their tracks and play from track 1. Cover-less titles get a placeholder that says BOOK, AUDIOBOOK or ALBUM instead of FILM.
- **Trailers on the first open.** The trailer list is kept for 7 days and survives restarts, and opening a title waits briefly for it, so Watch Trailer usually appears the first time. Trailers larger than 32–64 MiB now play: Ether picks a version that fits instead of failing (#672, #673, thanks @alcroito). Cast and crew also show on the first open of a movie.
- **Indexer identity by media type.** Compatibility rules can now apply to Movies, TV, Music or Books only, so movie requests can identify as Radarr and TV requests as Sonarr, for searches and downloads.
- **SubSource subtitles.** SubSource joins OpenSubtitles and SubDL under **Integrations › Subtitles**, with its official API key (#680, thanks @alcroito).
- **Plugin syncs you can see.** Sync and Full re-sync run in the background with live progress on the plugin page and its Activity tab. When a sync would remove more than half of a library, Ether keeps the items and asks whether to remove the missing ones, and items viewers opened, played or favourited are never pruned.

## Playback

- **Plex for Apple TV:** remuxed streams start at the beginning instead of the last few seconds (#686), and search returns results again (#687), thanks @Motley-Brew.
- **First play in the right language:** Plex for iOS direct play starts in your audio language on a title's first play (#670, thanks @kraigeisenman), and viewers who prefer original audio no longer have the right releases rejected (#674, thanks @firestaerter3).
- **Version picks stay put:** picking a version in Plezy keeps its real tracks and your subtitle rule, and a played version keeps its own entry instead of being promoted to Auto (#678, thanks @alcroito). The same release listed by two indexers shows once in the version menu.
- **Faster DASH start and resume**, and picked versions found in the vault without a new search (#636, thanks @drondeseries).
- **No more transcode retry storms:** a damaged segment is skipped or the release replaced, with backoff and hard limits, and retries stop once the viewer has left (#694, thanks @roadhazard).
- **Plex for iOS downloads resume** after the app is backgrounded instead of restarting from zero (#679), and downloads keep your audio and subtitle choice (#697), thanks @kraigeisenman.
- **Plex timeline** no longer refuses a position past the app's estimated duration (#696, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Samsung TVs play Plex Live TV**, including the native player's playlist refetches (#675, thanks @jmiller0), and Plex for Samsung no longer freezes on copied 4K streams.
- **A picked version finds a working copy:** when the version you picked fails, Ether tries another copy of the same quality and then steps down, never up to 4K.
- **Always convert video, per app:** an app or viewer that struggles with copied video can be set to always convert it (Playback › App compatibility, edit the app).
- **Android apps:** Findroid resumes at the right spot again instead of spinning, the official Jellyfin app direct plays and shows embedded subtitles, Silo applies subtitle, audio and quality changes and passes TrueHD and DTS-HD through to a receiver, Wholphin and Streamyfin direct play instead of converting, and switching titles or tracks stops the old stream right away.

## Libraries and apps

- **Collections pages open in seconds on large libraries:** a library's Collections tab in Vela, Jellyfin and Emby box sets, and Plex collections read only the page shown and no longer load every title of a large collection (such as a genre) to count it or show its poster. On a full TMDB catalogue the Movies tab went from about 30 seconds to about 3.
- **Rule-based libraries** (such as an Anime library) list only their own seasons and episodes again (#671, thanks @alcroito).
- **Custom collection rows show on Plex Home** for iOS and Apple TV again, collection edits reach every app's Home right away, and the Home editor says why a row is hidden for a viewer (not shared, no matching titles, restrictions, nothing playable yet, still building) (#710, thanks @xSkibbles).
- **Latest rows** in Jellyfin, Emby and Plex honour libraries built from collections (#668, thanks @firestaerter3).
- **Plex viewer import** finds accounts from connected servers on plex.tv before sharing, and saves them as linked Plex viewers (#681, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Live TV refreshes keep your channel settings** (screens, look and DVR minutes) (#695, thanks @kraigeisenman), and channels follow the provider's guide id when it changes unless you set your own (#706, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Unwatched filters** keep partly watched shows: a show counts as watched only when every aired episode is (#705, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Trakt sync** no longer sends shows and seasons to Trakt as movies (#707, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Vela Home** loads even when one of its rows is empty or no longer valid; that row is left out instead of the page failing.
- **Flows notification templates** create correctly, with their Send to picker (#677, thanks @machetie).

## Plugins

- **Plugins can use your language model** with a permission you approve: from actions and jobs (#669), and optionally during a viewer's search within the same time limit as Ether's own AI search (#702), thanks @FizzWhirl.
- **Parallel network calls** with `http.fetchMany`, and at most six requests at a time to any one site for every plugin (#703).
- **Faster, steadier plugin libraries:** browse totals cost nothing unless asked for (#682), re-indexing no longer holds the database writer (#685), playback lookups always keep a free plugin instance (#683), and hosted JavaScript plugins no longer leak memory on every call (#699).
- **Safer sources:** plugins get indexer links without API keys (#684), credentials follow redirects only to the same site or hosts the plugin names (#688), a release missing RAR volumes fails the Usenet check (#701), NZB file names with spaces are read whole (#700), a Usenet file a plugin picked by name keeps opening after its first play (#711), TorBox torrents with more than 100 files can be opened, including ones TorBox keeps only as a zip (#667, #709), source results say which debrid service holds each torrent (#704), a failed plugin source is never saved as the item's answer (#641), and plugin sources follow your **Cached torrents only** setting instead of always requiring a cached torrent (#708), thanks @FizzWhirl.
- **Stremio sources:** when some stream lookups fail, the search is reported as incomplete and not cached (#698, thanks @kraigeisenman).

## Under the hood

- Go and web dependencies are updated, including pgx 5.13 and Vite 8, and the Docker image builds on Node 26.

## Vela

Update Vela to **0.2.45 or later** (Apps › Vela) for faster section switching and library pages, native search on Apple TV, title pages labelled Book, Audiobook, Album or Song instead of Film, the eBook reader on phones and tablets, version lists that fill in when you open them, a visible message when a version can't be switched, Android TV keyboard and remote controls, and book, audiobook and album labels.
