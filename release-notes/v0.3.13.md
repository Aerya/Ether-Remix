# Ether v0.3.13

Highlights:

- **Opening a title is fast again.** Since 0.3.9, opening a show Ether had never seen could take 20–45 seconds, because the page filled in every season from TMDB while you waited. Now the first open makes one TMDB call and shows the page; the rest of the show fills in afterwards, in the background, and a season you jump to is fetched first. Pages Ether already knows answer from a handful of reads and never write.
- **Smoother debrid playback.** Starting, seeking and resuming read from the right place first, background reads step aside for the picture you're watching, and read-ahead keeps up with high-bitrate remuxes.
- **GPU tone mapping on Macs.** Eligible HDR-to-SDR conversions can decode, tone-map and scale on the GPU, with software fallback when the hardware path is unavailable.

## Fixes

- Artwork downloads and generated posters finish their cache writes before their owning request or worker exits, preventing writes after shutdown. Other viewers can retry a cancelled shared request.

- **Vela upgrades preserve open tabs.** Installs and rollbacks retain immutable chunks, player files and versioned skins. A release that reuses an immutable URL for different content is rejected before replacing the running build.

- **Activity tabs load without a catalogue-wide history scan.** History, Viewing and Delivery start their summary queries from the selected plays. History rows load independently of the charts, and Viewing and Delivery no longer fetch unused history rows.
- **Signs-only subtitles no longer count as full dialogue** ([#544](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/544)). Signs, songs and forced labels inform selection and automatic-download coverage. With normal subtitle preferences, a remembered forced choice yields to a full track in the same language on later episodes; explicit forced-only preferences and current manual selections remain respected.
- **Background preparation uses fewer NZB downloads.** Opening a title shares one small validation allowance across feed discovery and source selection. A warmup cannot spend that allowance again as new results arrive; pressing Play lifts the limit. A full preload shelf remembers sources it cannot fit instead of repeatedly resolving them ([#543](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/543), thanks @jmiller0).
- **Native PGS on supported Plex Android devices** ([#540](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/540), thanks @drondeseries). Native playback keeps image subtitles when the device supports them, while a required remux or a switch to HLS retains the burn-in fallback.
- **Optional direct TorBox delivery in Plex** ([#531](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/531)). Eligible original-file reads can go straight to TorBox; proxy delivery remains the default and handles requests that cannot redirect safely.
- **Direct Play audio appears in Now Playing** ([#539](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/539), thanks @drondeseries). When the client supplies no audio selection, the display uses the file's default audio track.
- **Plex Android Live TV navigation** ([#501](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/501)). Android gets DVR pivots, and channel shelves avoid duplicate entries. Other clients retain their existing single Live TV directory.

- **Ether starts even with a bad rule** ([#537](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/537), thanks @solohunter17). A library or viewer rule whose type was typed as "Movies" stopped 0.3.12 from starting. A rule Ether can't read no longer blocks startup: it is flagged in the console where you can fix it, and that viewer's restrictions stay on meanwhile. Type values like "Movies" or "TV Shows" are understood and stored the standard way, existing ones are converted, and the rule editor now offers a choice instead of free text.
- **Plex LG: 4K Play Version works** ([#514](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/514), thanks @alcroito). Auto no longer takes the only 4K release away from the 4K entry, a low-bitrate request from the app no longer swaps the source for a junk release, and a picked version that is temporarily unavailable is logged and retried as soon as a search finds it.
- **Alternate versions show their own subtitles** ([#461](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/461), thanks @kweaver5787). Whichever version you play, its embedded subtitle tracks are listed and selectable.
- **Older unfinished series stay in Continue Watching** ([#512](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/512), thanks @Aerya). A show you stopped months ago comes back with its next episode; movies keep the usual window.
- **TrueHD audio when Ether remuxes for Android TV** ([#523](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/523), thanks @drondeseries). TrueHD can't be copied into the segmented stream (it went silent), so Ether converts it to E-AC-3 there. Playing the original file still passes TrueHD through.
- **Live TV at 29.97 and 59.94 fps** ([#532](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/532), thanks @jmiller0). Continuous channels at NTSC frame rates no longer fail right after tuning.
- **Embedded subtitles on Plex for Android** ([#526](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/526), [#529](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/529), thanks @ggiannotti; refs [#191](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/191)). The selected embedded subtitle shows on the first play.
- **Detail pages keep their indexer allowance** ([#528](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/528), thanks @drondeseries). Background warm-ups and detail pages you open now have separate daily allowances, so background work can't use up what your detail pages need.
- **Docker bridge mode connects securely.** In bridge mode Ether only sees its container address, so app.plex.tv reported that the server can't connect securely. Add your host's LAN address under **Apps › Away from home › Custom server URLs** (for example `http://192.168.1.50:32400`) and Ether also publishes its secure plex.direct form.
- **Xtream logins keep their server address.** After a refresh, **Apps › IPTV apps (Xtream)** still shows the full server URL to enter in IPTV apps, not only the username and password.
- **Samsung TV apps get a Plex-shaped DASH stream** (refs [#483](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/483)). TV apps that play DASH get one video and one audio track with Plex's segment timing, and no stream ever mixes codecs.

## Libraries

- **Delete any library, including Movies and TV Shows.** The initial libraries are ordinary preseeded libraries. Deleting a library removes its published sections and prevents new playback through it while keeping media and watch history. Already authorized playback continues.
- **Plugin libraries stay deleted.** Plugin refreshes and restarts respect deletion. Add Library explicitly restores a selected plugin library.
- Upgrade every serving node before using library deletion; older versions do not honor the new deletion state.

## Vela and trailers

- **Open Vela tabs survive app updates and rollbacks.** Ether retains immutable app chunks, fonts, skins and player assets so an existing tab can continue loading its original build.
- **Vela details fill in without reopening the page.** First opens fetch the requested show's season list, and responses explicitly identify seasons, episodes or extras still loading. Partial responses cannot be cached; the client can refresh until the page is complete.
- **Vela playback stays alive while connected.** An authorized session event stream keeps local playback active even during long buffered stretches. Explicit stops still win. Cluster specialist playback keeps its existing behavior.
- **Vela browsing and search.** Home hydration has bounded query work, episode cards can use their series poster, explicit search scope is preserved, and discovery contention is retried.
- **Trailers work with split video and audio formats.** Ether muxes them into its shared seekable trailer cache.

## Devices

- **Seen devices** ([#522](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/522), thanks @drondeseries). **Playback › Devices** lists the devices your viewers actually played on; one click opens the override drawer filled in.
- **Force a device override** ([#524](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/524), [#538](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/538), thanks @drondeseries). An override can now win over what the app reports, for boxes that play more than their app admits. With Dolby Vision profile 7 forced, a Shield direct plays a DV7 remux with TrueHD intact.

## Episodes and releases

- **Releases numbered the way release groups number them.** For long-running anime and other shows where TMDB and release groups disagree (Inuyasha's S01E28 is S02E01 in releases), Ether uses the community scene and TVDB numbering automatically to find and match releases, while your library keeps TMDB's order. **Metadata › Episodes** can override it per show.

## Plugins

- **Hosted apps can serve cached files and archive members** ([#541](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/541), thanks @FizzWhirl). An adapter can return a blob descriptor and Ether streams the authorized bytes with byte-range support. Local redirects are supported too.
- **Plugin upgrades remount their apps** ([#542](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/542), thanks @FizzWhirl). Replacing an adapter now retires its previous listener so the updated app can start without restarting Ether.

- **Archives** ([#530](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/530), [#535](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/535), thanks @FizzWhirl). Reading many files from one archive no longer re-reads its index every time, and one file with an unusual name no longer makes the whole archive unreadable.
- **First run at install** ([#525](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/525)). A scheduled job runs right after the plugin is installed, then keeps its cadence across restarts.
- **Slow sources and verification** ([#533](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/533), [#534](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/534)). A resolver can answer "not ready yet" and Ether asks again; `http.fetch` takes a timeout; `http.hash` computes a file's digest on the server, in the background.
- **Library Rules use a plugin's own fields** ([#521](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/521)), and **LAN-only plugin apps mount** ([#520](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/520)).

## Under the hood

- Progressive playback bounds stalled client writes and releases the encoder when delivery fails.
- Live HLS playlists keep a constant target duration across reloads, based on completed startup media. A later segment that cannot fit the declared target triggers source recovery instead of publishing an invalid playlist.

- Hardware tone mapping for burned-in image subtitles.
- The API drift check runs again ([#509](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/509)).
