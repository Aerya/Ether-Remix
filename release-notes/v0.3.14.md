# Ether v0.3.14

Highlights:

- **Vela is here, on by default.** Ether's own app is on in every install. Ether downloads the signed stable Vela web player on first start, verifies it against the key built into Ether, and serves it at `/vela`, and **Watch** in the console header opens it already signed in. Vela for Windows, macOS and Apple TV talks to Ether's own protocol: heroes, person pages and filmographies, each library's Recommended, Collections and Genres views, and direct play. Updates and rollbacks stay one click in **Apps › Vela**, and browser tabs that are already open keep working through both. An owner who doesn't want Vela turns it off there.
- **Live TV, reorganised.** **Channels** is a new workspace:
  - **Showing for** chips (All apps, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Silo, IPTV apps, M3U, Vela) show and edit exactly what each app sees.
  - **Your groups** gather channels from any provider, such as Sports, News or Kids. They have drag-to-reorder and per-app switches, and channels a provider adds later follow their group's choices.
  - The list filters to enabled or disabled channels.

  - **Rules for groups:** a group can collect channels by name, provider group, guide ID, number or source, with contains, is, starts with, or a regular expression (#553). A live preview shows the matching channels.
  - **Backup feeds merge into one channel:** a channel's "Merge matching feeds" rules attach the same channel from other providers as backups, ordered by the provider priority you drag in **Sources › IPTV**, then by stream quality (#554).

  **Guide** opens at 3 hours, with what's on now above the grid and one toolbar for apps, groups, day and zoom. The old Guides tab is gone, and your guides became groups. In Plex, each of your groups is its own Live TV source.
- **The plugin marketplace is open.** **Plugins › Marketplace** now lists the official, signed catalogue from [github.com/braedonsaunders/ether-plugins](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether-plugins), and installs from it in one click. Anyone can submit a plugin with a pull request; CI checks the package and a maintainer reviews it. `ether plugin publish --url …` writes the entry for you. Each launch plugin was checked on a real server: Canada Live, Pluto TV, Local folders, TVmaze, Movie Night, October After Dark and Ether Wrapped. Plugins can also reach hosts on your own network that you allow per plugin (**On your network** in its settings), such as a Tuliprox proxy.
- **IPTV apps can play TS.** Xtream apps that ask for `.ts` streams, like iMPlayer or other ExoPlayer-based Android players set to TS, now get one continuous MPEG-TS stream instead of an HLS playlist. Apps that drop the `/xtream` part of the server URL also connect, because the standard Xtream paths now answer at the server root.

## Playback

- **Transcodes on Windows no longer stall every few seconds.** Ether stopped ffmpeg whenever it got ahead of the player and started a fresh one moments later, because Windows had no way to pause it. A remote 4K transcode restarted 24 times in five minutes. Ether now pauses and resumes ffmpeg on Windows as it does on Linux and macOS.
- **Lowering quality in a Plex app switches to a release that fits.** When a viewer picks a lower quality during playback, Ether now plays the release chosen for that bitrate. It used to keep transcoding the larger file. A release that already fits the cap streams without a video re-encode.
- **Samsung TVs play DASH.** Samsung's built-in player asks for the DASH manifest a second time without the Plex token. Ether now answers that one follow-up for the same session, from the same address, within two minutes (#483).
- **Channel logos show in Plex for iOS and iPadOS guides.**
- **"Too large for your connection" says how large.** The release explanation now gives the release's bitrate and the connection it was measured against.
- **Later seasons of long anime play.** When TMDB keeps a long show in one season (S01E25) but release groups number it as a new one (S03E01, "3rd Season - 01"), Ether now searches and matches the release groups' numbering from the anime mapping it already has (#559).
- **Good 4K releases are no longer rejected as truncated.** When Ether only had an estimate of a RAR volume's size, it could reject a correct archive header as "runs past end of volume". The header now wins until the exact size is known.
- **Bait and non-video files never play.** Shortcut and executable files (`.lnk`, `.exe`, double extensions like `.mkv.lnk`), soundtracks, ebooks and audiobooks are rejected before probing. Episode files implausibly small or large for their season pack are skipped. In a multi-file pack, the file in your profile's Audio language is preferred.
- **A crashed player leaves Now Playing in seconds.** Vela's live connection now detects a dead app within seconds and ends its session about 15 seconds after the app disappears.

## Libraries and Home

- **Show or hide specific titles.** Library rules have a new **Specific titles** condition. Pick individual movies or shows, then Show them as exceptions above a Hide rule, or Hide them outright.
- **Recommendations stay inside custom libraries.** A custom Plex library no longer recommends titles outside it (#549).
- **Pinned Plex libraries no longer repeat every row.** With Movies and TV both pinned to Plex Home, each library shows only its own rows instead of the household rows twice.
- **Plugin music libraries work in Plex.** A plugin music library shows its artists on Recommended, and artists open to albums and tracks instead of failing (#558).
- **Plugin library filters read alphabetically.** Labels sort by name, an enum follows its declared order, numbers keep the most common first, and a cut-off list says so (#555).
- **Browsing in a library's rules loads one page at a time.** The Browse drawer kept loading every title (tens of thousands) and refreshing. It now loads as you scroll. Checkboxes on posters lost their heavy border.
- **A row can keep titles other rows show.** In a Home row's settings, **Titles other rows show** offers Skip (the default) or Keep. Keep shows the row in full, such as an Apple TV Top 10 under a full Trending row, without taking any titles from the other rows.

## Console

- **Descriptive search shows the AI results first.** In Plex Web, Plezy, the Plex apps and Jellyfin, a search like "90s heist movies" waits up to about 10 seconds for the AI and leads with its results; a title search stays instant. The full result is also offered as a collection.
- **Clear cache clears everything.** With nothing playing, it now also removes the openings kept for unfinished titles, and no longer reports "Something went wrong".
- **Live TV can use its own proxy route.** **How Ether reaches the internet** has a separate switch for Live TV and IPTV, so only Live TV can go through a SOCKS5 proxy.
- **IPTV setup is quicker.** An M3U playlist whose header names its guide uses it automatically, and an Xtream login can be pasted as one URL.
- **Now Playing opens the stream.** Clicking a stream on Activity or Overview opens a drawer with what is playing and how: source and output video and audio, direct play or transcode and why, bitrate, release, device and buffering so far.
- **Cache explorer rows open a drawer** showing what is cached, where it came from, why it is kept and when it expires, with pin and clear actions.
- **Cache preloading settings live on Preloading.** Every warming and preloading option moved from Edit policy to the Preloading tab. Edit policy keeps storage: budget, retention and location. Inputs match the rest of the console.

## Performance

- **A Plex library page is a handful of queries.** One 50-title page with collections could run tens of thousands of database statements and time out. It now takes about 20–160 statements and under 50 ms on a 20,000-title library.

- **Plex Home loads in a fraction of the time.** A config copy on every request made the promoted hubs take up to 2 seconds of CPU. They now take about 0.2 seconds (#547).
- **Library sync and monitoring tools no longer spend your indexer budget.** Tracearr, Tautulli, Kometa and python-plexapi read metadata to mirror a library, and Ether treated every read as a viewer about to press Play. One sync spent a day's background indexer budget in 40 minutes. Batched availability checks and these tools now answer from what Ether already knows. Any client reading more detail pages than a person could gets no speculative work until it goes quiet (#551, thanks @zab1996).

## Plugins

- **Plugins read archives fast.** `archive.read` reads up to 4,096 members per call. The per-call read size follows the plugin's memory (a quarter of it, 8 MiB to 512 MiB). Reading a member from a cached archive dropped from about 158 ms to 0.2 ms (#552).
- **Plugins decode text natively.** `TextDecoder`/`TextEncoder` and a native UTF-8 codec decode a 37 KiB JSON member in about 1.6 ms instead of 30–60 ms (#556).
- **Plugins can extract archive members to their cache.** `archive.extract` streams a ZIP, 7z or RAR member into the plugin's library cache in one pass and returns its size, SHA-1, MD5 and CRC32, so records that carry only SHA-1 can be verified (#548).

- **Live TV plugins add their channels however they are installed.** Before, only Plugin Studio installs created the channels.
- **Archive limits measure what is really held** (#545). **The plugin check uses the plugin's own runtime limits** (#546).
- **Media route errors say what went wrong.** `kind`-media requests return a specific code and message: unknown ticket, viewer not allowed, item not found, no source, source unavailable or open failed. They used to return "not found" for every failure (#550).

## Fixes

- **Unraid installs start.** The Unraid template now defaults `/config` to the cache pool. The old `/mnt/user` default is a user share Ether refuses for its database.
- **Docker shows Ether's health.** `ether healthcheck` and a container `HEALTHCHECK` report whether the server is up.
- **Support bundles hide tracker passkeys,** including passkeys inside magnet links.
- **Prowlarr and Jackett are not flooded.** Ether sends at most eight requests at a time to one indexer host and recovers gradually after an outage.
- **Plex search is fast with a large Live TV guide.** A common word like "office" matched thousands of guide programmes, and each match scanned every channel, so a search took 5–25 seconds. It now takes about 0.15 seconds.

- **Tom and Jerry Tales and other split-episode shows play the right episode.** TMDB lists each short as its own episode, while many releases number by broadcast episode, so "S01E04" could mean two different things. Ether now skips a release whose name gives the title of a different episode of the season. It also drops wrong matches it already picked the next time you play.
- **Live TV keeps its lineup when a provider has a bad moment.** An Xtream or M3U provider that answers with no channels during an outage no longer wipes your lineup and channel settings. The refresh is marked failed and Ether tries again on the next one.
- **Gemini 3 works with AI search.** Ether now sends Gemini's `thoughtSignature` back with tool calls, which Gemini 3 models require on every follow-up turn.
- **Watchlist prewarm covers titles already on watchlists.** Prewarm used to warm a title only at the moment it was added to a watchlist. Titles already on watchlists are now queued too, newest first.
