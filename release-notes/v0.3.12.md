# Ether v0.3.12

Highlights:

- **Vela, Ether's own player.** The console's **Watch** button now opens Vela, with a small menu to choose Jellyfin web instead (each browser remembers the choice). Vela plays original files directly, including 4K, HEVC 10-bit and Dolby Vision. When a device needs conversion it uses a lazy quality ladder from the source resolution down: 4K HDR, 4K SDR, 1080p at 20/12/8 Mbps, 720p, 480p and 360p, each shown with the bandwidth it needs. Vela also has rating badges, full trailers, theme music, built-in skins with a **Default skin** choice in the console, and a sign-in screen that uses your skin.
- **Faster everywhere you press Play.** A ready cached copy no longer waits behind slower Usenet health checks. Opening a title in Vela prepares playback in the background. The first video piece after a start or seek is short (half a second when converting). Seeks report the true keyframe start, so audio and picture stay in step.
- **Live TV starts faster and fails clearly.** The startup burst now builds a real buffer, and the "Tonight" title gives way the moment the programme is ready. Channels with dozens of add-on links try several at once, best first. A channel whose sources are all down shows a plain error ("The channel source isn't responding") within seconds instead of spinning. **Playback › Streams & transcoding › Live TV** has a latency choice: Automatic, Prefer low delay or Prefer stability.
- **Bedtime.** **Allowed times** in a viewer's Limits tab now sets a bedtime, a wake time and days. The current programme finishes, then nothing new starts until wake time, in every app. The owner is never affected.

## Live TV

- **A rating for channels without one** ([#500](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/500), thanks @Bigboy1137). Each guide and IPTV source has **Rating for channels without one**, so profiles with a rating limit can see unrated IPTV channels you vouch for. Unset guides still hide unrated channels from limited profiles, and channels Ether recognises as adult never inherit it.
- **What's On shelves and guide categories in Plex** ([#501](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/501), thanks @drondeseries). Plex's Live TV now has What's On shelves built from your guide's own categories, category filters in the guide, channel-number ordering including subchannels, and optional shelves per IPTV channel group.
- **Jellyfin web's Live TV works.** The guide loads, and channels tune.
- **Unstable sources and audio.** Channels ride out sources that change layout mid-stream, and pick the best version that has sound.

## Plex

- **Seasons show from search on Fire TV** ([#468](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/468), thanks @eddieaoneill-sketch). Ether no longer rejects the extra option Plex for Fire TV sends when it opens a show's seasons.
- **Automatic subtitles download for Plex apps** ([#459](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/459), thanks @arjanterheegde). Plex echoing back the server's own subtitle choice no longer counts as a manual pick that blocks your "always download" rule. Titles played before this update may need their subtitle choice reset once.
- **Top 10 badges on Apple TV.** Ranked posters now keep their rank when Plex loads them through the image resizer.
- **Pins are respected.** A release you pin plays, converted if the device needs it, or fails with a clear reason. It's never silently swapped for another release.

## Jellyfin and Emby

- **Continue Watching, Next Up and seasons load in milliseconds** on large libraries, instead of tens of seconds.

## Subtitles

- **Searches use your languages** ([#504](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/504), thanks @DarkyGuld). SubDL and OpenSubtitles return only the viewer's preferred subtitle languages, or the language the app asks for.
- **Searches match the release you're playing** ([#505](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/505), thanks @DarkyGuld). SubDL receives the playing file's name, OpenSubtitles its hash and size, and exact release matches rank first.

## Releases

- **Language tags no longer stick to titles** ([#506](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/506), thanks @DarkyGuld). A release name without a year or resolution (`Terkel.I.Knipe.NORWEGIAN.DivX.DVDRip`) parses to the right title, so it isn't rejected as a mismatch.
- **The right film from multi-film packs**, and probes of a release are reused after a debrid link renews.

## Plugins

- **`archive.list` and `archive.open` read 7z and RAR** ([#502](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/502), thanks @FizzWhirl), with the same safety limits as ZIP and bounded ranged reads. The Reference explains the cost of random reads in solid archives.

## Console

- **Setup resumes where it stopped.** "Continue setup" opens the first missing step, and a server with every step done finishes setup quietly, without a banner.

## Under the hood

- Dependency updates: Vitest 5, jsdom 31, jest-dom 7, the Vite React plugin 5, Node 25 in Docker, plus Go and Actions updates ([#492](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/492)–[#499](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/499)).
- Ether's Jellyfin side logs each request at Debug level, which makes client problems easier to diagnose.
