# Ether v0.3.12

Highlights:

- **Faster everywhere you press Play.** A ready cached copy no longer waits behind slower Usenet health checks. The first video piece after a start or seek is short (half a second when converting).
- **Live TV starts faster and fails clearly.** The startup burst now builds a real buffer, and the "Tonight" title gives way the moment the programme is ready. Channels with dozens of add-on links try several at once, best first. A channel whose sources are all down shows a plain error ("The channel source isn't responding") within seconds instead of spinning. **Playback › Streams & transcoding › Live TV** has a latency choice: Automatic, Prefer low delay or Prefer stability.
- **Bedtime.** **Allowed times** in a viewer's Limits tab now sets a bedtime, a wake time and days. The current programme finishes, then nothing new starts until wake time, in every app. The owner is never affected.

## Live TV

- **A rating for channels without one** ([#500](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/500), thanks @Bigboy1137). Each guide and IPTV source has **Rating for channels without one**, so profiles with a rating limit can see unrated IPTV channels you vouch for. Unset guides still hide unrated channels from limited profiles, and channels Ether recognises as adult never inherit it.
- **What's On shelves and guide categories in Plex** ([#501](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/501), thanks @drondeseries). Plex's Live TV now has What's On shelves built from your guide's own categories, category filters in the guide, channel-number ordering including subchannels, and optional shelves per IPTV channel group.
- **Jellyfin web's Live TV works.** The guide loads, and channels tune.
- **Unstable sources and audio.** Channels ride out sources that change layout mid-stream, and pick the best version that has sound.

- **Switching channels on a one-connection IPTV provider works.** The channel you just left no longer holds the provider's only connection for a minute, so the next channel starts instead of failing with "provider busy". A channel someone is still watching is never cut off.
- **Pick channel groups faster.** In a source's **Channel groups**, shift-click selects a range, and **Select all** / **Select none** act on whatever the search shows. One Save applies everything.

## IPTV apps (Xtream)

- **Short logins you can type on a remote.** New logins are a short username (the viewer's name plus two digits, like `sam42`) and a 10-character password with no look-alike characters, shown as `k7m2-9qxr-4t`. Dashes and capitals don't matter when typing. Existing logins keep working.
- **See logins again.** The owner console keeps each new login's password hidden behind a show button, with copy buttons, so you can give it out again later. Only the owner can reveal it. Logins made before this update can't be shown; create a new one.
- **Multi-view.** All of a viewer's Xtream logins share that viewer's stream limit instead of one stream each, and each login can have an optional **Streams on this device** limit. Ether applies whichever is lower and tells the app, so players like TiviMate can show two games at once. At the limit, a new stream on the same device replaces that device's oldest one, so channel switching never waits.

## Plex

- **Seasons show from search on Fire TV** ([#468](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/468), thanks @eddieaoneill-sketch). Ether no longer rejects the extra option Plex for Fire TV sends when it opens a show's seasons.
- **Automatic subtitles download for Plex apps** ([#459](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/459), thanks @arjanterheegde). Plex echoing back the server's own subtitle choice no longer counts as a manual pick that blocks your "always download" rule. Titles played before this update may need their subtitle choice reset once.
- **Top 10 badges on Apple TV.** Ranked posters now keep their rank when Plex loads them through the image resizer.
- **Embedded subtitles on Plex for Android and iOS** ([#515](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/515), thanks @ggiannotti; refs [#191](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/191)). Those apps build their subtitle list from the first detail response and never ask again, so Ether now waits up to the page's own allowance (3.5 s instead of 1.5 s) for the file's track list. A slower check still answers on time.
- **Embedded subtitles draw on Plex for Android** ([#511](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/511), thanks @ggiannotti; refs [#191](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/191)). The Android player no longer drops the subtitle track muxed into the stream, so a subtitle you pick is shown, not just listed.
- **A played title keeps its track list** ([#518](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/518), thanks @ggiannotti). When the release search is briefly unavailable, the detail page still lists the audio and subtitle tracks of the release you last played.
- **Top 10 rows show their artwork in Plex Home.** Ranked rows with upcoming titles showed blank tiles, wide or poster-shaped. Wide cards now show the backdrop with its rank and fall back to the ranked poster when there is no backdrop.
- **Pins are respected.** A release you pin plays, converted if the device needs it, or fails with a clear reason. It's never silently swapped for another release.

## Hardware transcoding

- **Formats your GPU can't decode keep the hardware encoder** ([#516](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/516), thanks @alcroito). Ether checks once, in the background, which formats your GPU can actually decode. Anything else (like MPEG-4 Part 2 AVI on many Intel GPUs) is decoded in software and still encoded on the GPU, instead of failing once and falling back to the CPU. This covers VAAPI, QSV, NVIDIA and Apple.

## Jellyfin and Emby

- **Continue Watching, Next Up and seasons load in milliseconds** on large libraries, instead of tens of seconds.

## Subtitles

- **Searches use your languages** ([#504](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/504), thanks @DarkyGuld). SubDL and OpenSubtitles return only the viewer's preferred subtitle languages, or the language the app asks for.
- **Searches match the release you're playing** ([#505](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/505), thanks @DarkyGuld). SubDL receives the playing file's name, OpenSubtitles its hash and size, and exact release matches rank first.

## Releases

- **Language tags no longer stick to titles** ([#506](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/506), thanks @DarkyGuld). A release name without a year or resolution (`Terkel.I.Knipe.NORWEGIAN.DivX.DVDRip`) parses to the right title, so it isn't rejected as a mismatch.
- **The right film from multi-film packs**, and probes of a release are reused after a debrid link renews.

- **TorBox and other debrid providers never pick a text file to play** ([#507](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/507), thanks @DarkyGuld). Only real video files are chosen, even when a request names a specific file; a release without one is skipped instead of spinning.
- **Hyphenated and joined titles match** ([#519](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/519), thanks @alcroito). K-Pop/KPop, Spider-Man/Spiderman, X-Men/XMen and JoJo.s/JoJo's no longer reject valid releases.
- **Titles in your language** ([#508](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/508), thanks @DarkyGuld). Norwegian (nb) and other macrolanguage settings now get TMDB's translated titles, and your language's alternative titles are kept first, so dubbed releases named in your language match. Existing titles pick this up on their next normal metadata refresh.

## Plugins

- **`archive.list` and `archive.open` read 7z and RAR** ([#502](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/502), thanks @FizzWhirl), with the same safety limits as ZIP and bounded ranged reads. The Reference explains the cost of random reads in solid archives.

## Console

- **Live TV search sits in the page header**, right next to **Add**, on every Live TV tab, so it is never squeezed.
- **Setup resumes where it stopped.** "Continue setup" opens the first missing step, and a server with every step done finishes setup quietly, without a banner.

- **Audio codecs and containers in App compatibility** ([#517](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/517), thanks @alcroito). The app and device editors (Playback › App compatibility) can now override which audio codecs and containers an app plays directly, for example TrueHD on a receiver setup that handles it.
- **AI rule approvals keep working for shared profile names** ([#510](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/510), thanks @activechay-lab). Approving a proposed rule or format score no longer fails when a movie and a TV profile share a name.

## Under the hood

- Dependency updates: Vitest 5, jsdom 31, jest-dom 7, the Vite React plugin 5, Node 25 in Docker, plus Go and Actions updates ([#492](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/492)–[#499](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/499)).
- Ether's Jellyfin side logs each request at Debug level, which makes client problems easier to diagnose.
