# Ether v0.3.17

Highlights:

- **Flows: automate anything that happens on your server.** A new **Flows** page lets you build automations from 120 typed events across 14 areas (playback, library, viewers, Live TV, sources, plugins, system and more) with conditions, templates and actions. Start from one of 15 templates or describe a flow in plain words and let your configured AI draft it. You can test a draft against a real event before saving it, and every run keeps a step-by-step trace. Plugins can add their own events and actions, and AI apps can manage flows through MCP.
- **Music, books and audiobooks from plugins.** Plugin libraries can now be music (artist, album and track), books (with series) or audiobooks (single files with chapters, or ordered parts), and every app shows them as what they are. Jellyfin and Emby get proper MusicArtist, MusicAlbum, Audio, Book and AudioBook items, with working Artists, Album Artists and Music Genres views. Plex gets music libraries, and Vela 0.2.36 adds dedicated music, book and audiobook pages, an audio player and an eBook reader. Usenet and debrid releases can now serve audio and book files, including files inside RAR, 7z and ZIP archives. Plugins can also check a Usenet release's articles before offering it.
- **Plugins decide how a library looks, you decide where it appears.** A plugin's manifest now only describes how each app presents its library. Your **Show in apps** switches decide whether it is published there, and they survive plugin updates. When a manifest doesn't mention an app, Ether works out a sensible presentation from the library's shape. When an app has no fitting library type (books on Plex), the switch says why.
- **Honest indexer identity, with compatibility rules.** Ether always identifies itself as `Ether/<version>` by default, with a link to a new page for indexer operators: what Ether requests and when, the limits it respects, and that no AI is involved in indexer requests. For indexers that only accept specific apps, **Sources › Indexers › How Ether identifies itself** lets you add rules for one indexer, a host, all searches or all NZB/torrent downloads, including downloads from feeds and Stremio add-ons. Each rule shows exactly what the indexer will see. Compatibility rules change headers only, never the shape of the request.
- **One upload limit for every remote stream.** Ether now measures how much upload capacity your connection really has from its own remote streams, and keeps all remote streams together within about 85% of it. New streams get a fitting bitrate, adaptive streams step down when someone joins and back up when they leave, and streams on your home network are never affected. If your Plex Media Server is linked to the same Plex account, its remote streams count too. You can set your upload speed yourself under **Quality › Away from home**, and Activity shows when the shared limit capped a stream (#658, thanks @fatbob01).
- **Playback keeps trying until it finds a release that works.** Starting playback no longer gives up after four releases: Ether keeps trying within the start-up time limit and races alternatives three at a time once the first one fails. When several releases agree on a runtime that the catalogue gets wrong, Ether trusts the releases and corrects the catalogue. More multi-part RAR layouts now play.

## Playback

- **First-play subtitles in Plezy.** On a never-played episode or movie, Plezy started with subtitles off and only followed your subtitle rule on the second play. Ether now recognises Plezy's playback request and answers with the real tracks and your rule's subtitle selected, from the detail page, the season list, Home and hub rows. Browsing never waits (#618, thanks @bmkhib).
- **Android apps:** Ether was tested against eight Android clients on a real tablet, and these were fixed:
  - Dolby Vision profile 8 now direct-plays in Streamyfin and similar apps.
  - Fladder and Wholphin no longer get forced to SDR.
  - HEVC is remuxed for TS-only Android players instead of being re-encoded to 4K H.264.
  - Jellyfin playlists now cover the whole title, which fixes wrong resume positions, seeks that landed an hour off, and audio switches that jumped back to the start.
  - Jellyfin for Android no longer crashes on resume.
  - TrueHD and DTS are never copied into segments that can't carry them.
- **Silo:**
  - Playback no longer fails with "Selected track unavailable" when your subtitle rule picks a plain SRT track.
  - A stale or server-chosen audio or subtitle track never stops a start, in every app.
  - Embedded subtitles show from the first line, and subtitle files for other apps are read from the file's index in about a second instead of 40 s or more.
  - Cold Dolby Vision + TrueHD files keep direct play.
- **Hardware decoding on Intel Quick Sync after upgrading.** A hardware-decode test failure saved by an older Ether was reused after upgrading, so HEVC kept decoding on the CPU. Ether now re-tests once after an update.
- **Plex Roku:**
  - Libraries open instead of crashing (#652, thanks @vicbyrd).
  - Playback no longer stops mid-episode.
  - A retry no longer restarts the stream.
  - Sound starts with the picture on resume (#650, thanks @roadhazard).
- **Plex for Fire TV offers both your NAS copy and Ether's again** (#654, thanks @eddieaoneill-sketch).
- **Plex /status/sessions reports the real stream after a quality change**, so Tautulli sees transcodes (#622, thanks @roadhazard).
- **Audio tracks:**
  - Ether always checks a release's audio languages, even when the title's original language matches yours.
  - Commentary and audio-description tracks rank below the main track (#664, thanks @drondeseries).
  - Dual-audio releases start in the right language on first play (#624, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Double-episode releases (S01E01-E02)** are accepted for shows that list them as single episodes (#634, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Trailers and extras:**
  - Plex Discover extras are used as a trailer source (#637, thanks @alcroito).
  - Trailers and extras can go through the outbound proxy (#638, thanks @alcroito).
- **Version choice:**
  - Pick a version and keep it, with a remembered choice per title.
  - Plugin items get the same version picker as movies and shows, in Vela and Plex.
- **The quality menu on direct-played files** shows the real quality options instead of a broken "0p" row.

## Plugins

- **Usenet works for every content type**, not only video (#659).
- **Plugins can:**
  - list a release's files, including files inside archives, and pick one, on Usenet as on debrid (#644);
  - check a Usenet release's articles (#665);
  - verify a release against a known digest (#642);
  - choose a source per stream link (#661);
  - hash files that need a login (#660);
  - rasterise SVG artwork (#630);
  - delete cache blobs (#631).
- **The plugin runtime is more robust:**
  - One slow or timed-out call no longer closes the plugin's runtime or fails its other calls (#628, #627).
  - A non-JSON request to a plugin frontend is no longer counted as a crash (#645).
  - Archive members with backslashes in their names can be read (#632).
  - Facets support "without" and "all of" (#626).
  - Playback-preparation jobs no longer wait for the server to go idle.
  - Range sources read ahead in windows that grow to 4 MiB, so a whole-file download through one is no longer one upstream request per 32 KiB (#666, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Large plugin libraries are fast:**
  - No more 8,000-item cap in Jellyfin, Emby and Plex.
  - Republishing unchanged items is skipped (#646).
  - Deleting items no longer scans the whole table (#647).
  - Smaller numeric indexes (#623).
  - SQLite's cache is sized from your server's memory (#643).
  - Installs return at once, with progress shown while the first sync runs in the background.
- **Library pages:**
  - Plugin libraries can have tabs and shelves at any level (Albums, Artists).
  - "Recently played" shelves only show what you have played.
  - Continue rows are titled Watching, Reading or Listening as fits.
  - Opening an artist or album fills it in on demand.
- **Faster Plex browsing:** a section's episode list now costs a fixed handful of queries per page instead of one per episode (#639, thanks @alcroito).

## Sources and indexers

- **Usenet file sizes and torrent results:** release file lists report real decoded sizes, and torrent results from Cardigann indexers are typed as torrents.
- **Stremio add-ons can keep their own result order** (#625, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **MDBList lists import up to 5,000 titles** (#657, thanks @firestaerter3).

## Apps and console

- **App addresses:**
  - Every link Ether hands out uses the address the request actually came in on, whether a subdomain, a path or a port.
  - The console shows the addresses each app was really used on.
  - An optional **Published addresses** list is used for invites and QR codes.
- **Vela and the console can run on their own ports**, so a reverse proxy can give them their own HTTPS addresses, and you can stop serving them on the Plex port.
- **Remote client IPs:** Ether now reports them correctly behind a reverse proxy on another host (#635, thanks @roadhazard).
- **Jellyfin API:** `AnyProviderIdEquals` and `IsPlayed` are honoured on `/Users/{id}/Items` (#656, thanks @firestaerter3).
- **LLM and MCP:**
  - OpenAI-compatible LLMs get a bounded reply size, which fixes OpenRouter 404s (#649, thanks @firestaerter3).
  - MCP sign-in accepts the space-separated scope `read full` (#651, thanks @firestaerter3).
- **SQLite:** a valid sign-in is never refused because a bookkeeping write is slow (#648).
- **Images:**
  - Plex posters are scaled to the size the app asks for.
  - Backdrops and stills requested without a size use a TV-sized image.
  - Expired image tags no longer blank posters in Jellyfin and Emby apps.
- **Plex Home profiles** show avatars and restrictions, and Vela shows each viewer's Plex avatar.
- **Indexer searches find titles with colons and accents.** Title searches drop punctuation release names never carry and also search accented titles unaccented, like Sonarr and Radarr.
- **Activity shows only what is really playing.** Sessions that stopped reporting, or were prepared and never played, end on their own and no longer count as active streams.
- **Plex people:** actors open their Ether pages instead of an error (#653, thanks @roadhazard).
- **Catalogue fixes:** a stuck rank view after a restore is fixed (#633, thanks @oleedv), and title sorting ignores leading punctuation and "The/A/An".

## Vela

Update Vela to **0.2.36 or later** (Apps › Vela) to use the new features below; older Vela versions keep working without them.

- Music, books and audiobooks pages, an audio player (queue, shuffle, repeat, speed, sleep timer, chapters) and an eBook reader (EPUB, PDF, CBZ).
- Viewer avatars, playlists, versions, Remove from Continue Watching, Plex Home profiles and Smart Views.
- Inline Live TV channel controls, and a steadier web player.
